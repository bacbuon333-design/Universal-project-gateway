## 1. SCIENTIFIC OBJECTIVE & CORE PHILOSOPHY
The goal of Quantitative Research V3.3 is to discover whether any simple, causal, economically plausible market mechanism can produce:
1. **Repeated opportunities across time** without multi-month inactivity;
2. **Hard temporal frequency**: $\ge 5$ trades in **EVERY** complete quarter (33/33 complete quarters from 2018Q2 to 2026Q2 on Gold M30);
3. **Distributed profitability**: PnL distributed smoothly across time rather than concentrated into a few windfall quarters;
4. **Cost-adjusted economic edge**: Positive expectancy net of 25 pips spread + $7/lot commission on Gold;
5. **Rolling consistency**: Rolling 4Q positive $\ge 70\%$ and Rolling 4Q $PF \ge 1.20 \ge 65\%$.

---

## 2. HARD ACCEPTANCE & REJECTION GATES (FROZEN STANDARD)

### Screen 1 — Causality & Execution Contract
- 100% causal signals computed on bar $i$ close; execution strictly on bar $i+1$ open.
- Pessimistic ambiguous-bar resolution (SL preferred).
- 1 position concurrency at a time.
- Dynamic asset-aware PnL net of spread, commission, and slippage.

### Screen 2 — Temporal Frequency Gate (Gate A1)
- Minimum trades per complete quarter $\ge 5$ across all 33 complete quarters.
- If ANY complete quarter has $0, 1, 2, 3,$ or $4$ trades $\implies$ **INSTANT REJECTION**. No exceptions.

### Screen 3 — Trade Distribution Gates (Gates A2–A4)
- **Gate A2**: Max single quarter trade share $\le 5.0\%$.
- **Gate A3**: Max quarterly trades / Median quarterly trades $\le 3.0$.
- **Gate A4**: Quarterly trade-count Gini $< 0.30$.

### Screen 4 — Raw Economic Edge Gates (Gates E1–E2)
- **Gate E1**: Full-sample Profit Factor $PF \ge 1.25$.
- **Gate E2**: Full-sample Net Expectancy $> \$0.00$.

### Screen 5 — Rolling Robustness Gates (Gates D1–D2)
- **Gate D1**: Rolling 4-quarter positive windows $\ge 70.0\%$.
- **Gate D2**: Rolling 4-quarter $PF \ge 1.20$ windows $\ge 65.0\%$.

### Screen 6 — Profit Distribution Gates (Gates B1–B2)
- **Gate B1**: Top 3 profitable quarters $\le 40.0\%$ of total positive net PnL.
- **Gate B2**: Top 5 profitable quarters $\le 60.0\%$ of total positive net PnL.
